Ball engineering

Obsessively round.
Relentlessly consistent.

01

One seamless cast

No seams, no welds, no weak points. Each ball is rotationally cast as a single piece, so it wears evenly and never splits at a joint — rally after rally, season after season.

02

40 precision-drilled holes

Machine-drilled to a ±0.1 mm tolerance and balanced around the seamless core. The result is a dead-true flight path that holds its line through wind and hard drives.

03

Weighted for the win

Tuned to the middle of the USA Pickleball spec window — enough mass to punch through a breeze, enough touch to drop a dink on the line. Approved for sanctioned play.

Our story

We made the ball
we couldn't buy.

Every ball on the shelf was molded overseas, split at the seam by August, and flew like it had opinions. So we did the unreasonable thing: built a foundry-grade casting line in Spokane, Washington, and started making pickleballs the way America used to make everything — properly.

Every MINA ball is cast, drilled, inspected, and packed within American borders by people who play on Tuesday nights. When you rally with MINA, you're keeping the game — and the work — at home.
