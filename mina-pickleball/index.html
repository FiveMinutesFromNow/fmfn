Our story

MINA is short for
Made in America.

Founded in 2024 and proudly headquartered in Georgia, MINA was born out of a passion for pickleball and a belief in American craftsmanship. Founders Jeffrey Dunn and Corey Singman set out to build a professional-grade ball that meets the highest standards for tournament play — manufactured entirely in the USA.

And the court is only the beginning. By partnering with domestic manufacturers, MINA aims to grow into a multi-category brand — sporting goods today; cookware, tools, and home essentials tomorrow — proving that world-class products can still be built with pride on American soil.
